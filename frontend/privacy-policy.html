1. Civic Identity & Data Controller

The data controller responsible for personal information processed through PowerWatch is the CheckLocal Civic Initiative, an open-source civic technology project developed for the Open Society Foundations (OSF) × Andela Hackathon 2026.

Our principal purpose is enabling retail electricity consumers to safely log power outages, verify tariff overcharges, and generate collective regulatory complaints without exposing themselves to retaliation from state or private monopoly utilities.

2. Statutory Privacy Compliance

PowerWatch processes all data in strict accordance with relevant data protection legislation across our operational jurisdictions:

  • Federal Republic of Nigeria: The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) enforced by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).
  • Republic of South Africa: The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) enforced by the Information Regulator.
  • Republic of Kenya: The Data Protection Act 2019 enforced by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).
  • International Standards: Core principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), specifically data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation, and integrity.

3. Information We Collect & Telemetry Scope

We strictly enforce the principle of data minimization. We only collect the bare minimum data required to establish evidentiary quorum for a power outage or tariff overcharge:

A. WhatsApp Bot Telemetry

  • Phone Number: When you send a message to our WhatsApp hotline (+234 812 243 2599), we receive your WhatsApp phone number. This number is immediately hashed using cryptographic salt in our backend and is never displayed in public feeds or dockets (e.g., displayed only as +234 812 *** **99).
  • Message Text & Timestamps: The content of your outage report and exact time of receipt.

B. Meter & Utility Telemetry

  • Meter Serial Number: Used solely to prevent Sybil attacks and verify that reported outages stem from unique physical premises. Meter numbers are stored with AES-256 encryption at rest and truncated in public dockets (e.g., ***4572).
  • Distribution Utility (DisCo): The electric utility serving your property (e.g., AEDC, IKEDC, EKEDC, IBEDC, City Power Johannesburg, Kenya Power).
  • Feeder & Band Classification: The 33kV/11kV feeder line and statutory tariff band (e.g., Band A, Band B).
  • Locality: Neighborhood, estate, or local government area. We do not collect real-time GPS coordinates, indoor floor plans, or exact street addresses.

C. Website Browsing (Zero Tracking Cookies)

  • Our website (powerwatch.up.railway.app) uses no advertising cookies, no Meta Pixel, no Google Analytics trackers, and no third-party tracking scripts.
  • Standard web server access logs (IP address, user agent) are retained temporarily for 14 days solely for DDoS defense and rate-limiting.

4. Zero Commercial Monetization Guarantee

Absolute Non-Commercial Guarantee: We will never sell, lease, monetize, license, or trade your personal data, phone number, or meter telemetry to commercial data brokers, direct marketing firms, debt collectors, or advertising exchanges.

All data collected by PowerWatch is used exclusively for civic watchdog purposes: evaluating utility service delivery, calculating regulatory tariff overcharges, and compiling statutory petitions.

5. The Retaliation Shield & De-Identification

In many developing electricity markets, retail consumers fear that filing individual complaints against monopoly utilities may result in punitive power cuts, targeted billing harassment, or delayed maintenance.

PowerWatch operates a built-in Retaliation Shield:

  • Collective Quorum Anonymization: Individual meter reports are aggregated into feeder-level statistical quorums. Public audits display only aggregated metrics (e.g., "14 independent meters on Magodo 33kV recorded 6.8 hrs average supply").
  • Subpoena Docket Masking: When formal dockets are submitted to NERC or NERSA, citizen phone numbers are replaced with anonymous cryptographic hashes (e.g., CITIZEN-REF-89B4), with original numbers held in secure escrow only accessible upon statutory court subpoena.

6. Lawful Grounds for Processing

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (Section 25) and POPIA (Section 11), PowerWatch processes your telemetry under the following legal bases:

  • Explicit Consent: Provided when you initiate a report via our WhatsApp bot hotline or submit a meter audit through our web portal.
  • Public Interest & Consumer Protection: Processing aggregated electricity supply telemetry to protect retail consumers against unlawful billing surcharges under Section 63 of the Electricity Act 2023 and the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) 2018.

7. Data Storage, Encryption & Retention

Storage & Security: All telemetry is stored in secure relational databases hosted on ISO 27001 / SOC 2 compliant cloud infrastructure (Railway Cloud). All database connections are enforced over TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, and sensitive meter attributes are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

Retention Schedule:

  • Active Regulatory Quorum Data: Retained for 90 days following a verified tariff breach to support regulatory filing before the NERC Forum or NERSA.
  • Historical Feeder Analytics: Permanently stripped of all phone numbers and meter serial numbers; converted into pure anonymized historical time-series data for civic research.
  • Raw WhatsApp Webhook Logs: Automatically purged after 30 days.

8. Your Data Subject Rights

Under NDPA, POPIA, and GDPR, you possess the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may immediately delete all your stored telemetry and phone records by sending the keyword DELETE MY DATA to our WhatsApp bot (+234 812 243 2599), or by emailing privacy@checklocal.org.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct your registered meter number, tariff band, or feeder details at any time by messaging UPDATE METER on WhatsApp.
  • Right of Access: You may request a machine-readable copy of all outage logs associated with your hashed phone identifier.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may opt out of automated feeder broadcast updates at any time by messaging STOP.

9. Third-Party Infrastructure Processors

PowerWatch utilizes select enterprise infrastructure providers to operate our civic watchdog services:

  • Meta Cloud API (WhatsApp Business): Processes incoming and outgoing WhatsApp messages pursuant to Meta's Business Data Processing Terms.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini AI API): Evaluates conversational intent and categorizes utility tariff breaches using regional civic heuristics. No personal phone numbers or unredacted meter numbers are shared with model training pipelines.
  • Railway Cloud: Hosts our containerized FastAPI backend and PostgreSQL database infrastructure.
