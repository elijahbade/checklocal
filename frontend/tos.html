2. Non-Commercial Mission & Hackathon Foundation

PowerWatch is a public-interest, non-commercial civic technology platform developed for the Open Society Foundations (OSF) × Andela Hackathon 2026 under the "Information you can trust" (Transparency & Accountability) track.

Public Interest Covenant: PowerWatch is operated strictly for consumer protection, public accountability, and regulatory enforcement. We do not sell consumer telemetry, meter data, or location patterns to commercial data brokers, advertising networks, or credit rating agencies.

Our objective is to bridge the evidentiary asymmetry between retail electricity consumers and monopoly distribution companies (DisCos / municipal utilities) by compiling ground-truth citizen logs into verifiable regulatory complaint dockets.

3. Crowdsourced Telemetry & Truthfulness

When submitting reports to PowerWatch via WhatsApp, web forms, or automated telemetry, you agree to:

  • Provide factual, unmanipulated meter serial numbers, tariff classifications (Band A, B, C, D, or E), and utility names (e.g., AEDC, EKEDC, IBEDC, City Power).
  • Report accurate timestamps for power outage occurrences and restoration events.
  • Refrain from submitting fabricated, automated, or malicious reports intended to artificially manipulate a feeder's statutory compliance score.

PowerWatch utilizes automated heuristic screening and anomaly detection (powered by Google Gemini and geographical validation models) to filter out duplicate, implausible, or uncorroborated submissions.

4. Quorum Corroboration & Regulatory Dockets

PowerWatch does not publish individual, isolated consumer complaints as verified truth. To guarantee subpoena-grade credibility, a feeder-level tariff breach is only marked as Verified Quorum when:

  1. Multiple independent meters connected to the identical 33kV/11kV distribution feeder corroborate the supply deficit over the statutory evaluation window (typically 24 hours to 7 consecutive days).
  2. The recorded hours of daily supply fall materially below the statutory guarantee mandated by the regulator (e.g., minimum 20.0 hours/day for Band A customers under the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Multi-Year Tariff Order).
  3. The calculated tariff surcharge reflects the differential between the premium Band A tariff (₦206.00–₦209.50/kWh) and the actual service delivered (e.g., Band D at ~₦70.70/kWh).
Statutory Legal Anchor: Under Section 63 of the Electricity Act 2023 and the NERC Customer Protection Regulations, utilities that fail to deliver committed feeder supply hours are legally liable to downgrade the feeder and credit overcharged consumers. In South Africa, the Pretoria High Court precedent (AfriForum v NERSA) prohibits unlawful municipal tariff surcharges.

5. Evidentiary Licensing & Data Use

By submitting telemetry, outage records, or utility bills to PowerWatch, you grant CheckLocal a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to:

  • Aggregate, de-identify, and anonymize your submission in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
  • Incorporate the aggregated data into public feeder audits, civic dashboards, and statistical research reports.
  • Generate formal, subpoena-grade regulatory complaint dockets submitted to statutory regulatory bodies, including the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) Forum Offices, the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC), and the National Energy Regulator of South Africa (NERSA).

You retain ownership of any original documents (e.g., copies of electricity tokens or bills) you provide. PowerWatch permanently redacts private identifiers before generating public dockets.

6. Prohibited Conduct & Anti-Spoofing

Users are strictly prohibited from engaging in any of the following activities:

  • Sybil Attacks & Synthetic Telemetry: Deploying bots, scripts, or burner numbers to flood the WhatsApp hotline with fake outage records.
  • Tampering with Verification Quorum: Attempting to inflate or deflate feeder reliability scores for commercial or extortionate purposes against any utility licensee.
  • Denial of Service (DoS): Flooding the API endpoints, web simulator, or Meta webhook servers.
  • Unauthorized Extraction: Scraping personal or non-public data from the platform for commercial marketing or unsolicited contact.

Violations of this section may result in permanent banning of the user's phone number and IP address, and referral to law enforcement under applicable cybercrime legislation (e.g., Cybercrimes Act 2015 of Nigeria).

7. Disclaimers & Emergency Limitations

Not an Electricity Distribution Licensee: PowerWatch is an independent civic watchdog. We do not generate, transmit, or distribute electrical power, nor do we operate grid switching equipment.

CRITICAL ELECTRICAL EMERGENCY NOTICE: PowerWatch is NOT an emergency dispatch service. If you observe fallen high-tension cables, transformer fires, electrical explosions, or life-threatening hazards, do NOT report them through our WhatsApp bot. Immediately contact your local distribution utility emergency line or civil defense emergency services.

Evidentiary Basis: While PowerWatch applies rigorous multi-meter corroboration and automated AI validation, all dockets, audits, and tariff calculations are provided for civic advocacy and regulatory complaint filing on an "as is" and "as available" basis without warranties of immediate regulatory enforcement.
