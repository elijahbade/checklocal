Accountability Architecture • OSF Values Grounding

Holding Powerful Monopoly Utilities Accountable

PowerWatch does not surveil citizens or sell data to commercial giants. Accountability flows strictly upwards: giving ordinary households and civil society coalitions the evidentiary leverage required to challenge monopoly DisCos and municipal distributors before regulatory tribunals and high courts.

⚡

Section 63 SCADA Subpoenas

Under Section 63 of the Electricity Act 2023, distribution licensees are statutorily required to maintain unaltered telemetry logs. When 10+ corroborated meter logs demonstrate Band A under-delivery, PowerWatch petitions compel NERC to subpoena the DisCo’s primary injection substation busbar telemetry.

💸

Mandatory Refunds & Downgrades

Precedents are real and binding: NERC fined AEDC ₦200,000,000 and issued regulatory orders forcing the downward reclassification and retrospective token crediting of over 557 underperforming feeder routes across 9 DisCos. PowerWatch scales this enforcement to every neighborhood.

⚖️

Pretoria High Court Precedent

In South Africa, the High Court ruled in AfriForum v NERSA that municipal electricity tariffs without approved Cost-of-Supply (CoS) studies are unconstitutional and invalid. PowerWatch equips township ratepayer collectives to legally contest unapproved surcharges.
