Accountability & Entrepreneurial Model

From Street-Level WhatsApp Rumors to Institutional Action

CheckLocal is not just a consumer utility. By aggregating verified ground truth from thousands of daily WhatsApp chats, we solve severe information asymmetries for regulatory watchdogs and commercial supply chains across emerging markets.

🏛️

B2G Regulatory Escalation

When stations hoard fuel or gouge prices, verified incident clusters are automatically dispatched to enforcement authorities: NMDPRA (Petroleum Regulator), FCCPC (Consumer Protection), and NERC (Electricity Commission).

📊

B2B FMCG Pricing Intelligence

Consumer goods giants (Nestlé, Unilever, Flour Mills) spend millions on quarterly surveys. CheckLocal licenses real-time, street-level retail price telemetry across peri-urban markets on high-margin API subscriptions.

𝕏

Automated Social Debunking

Viral falsehoods that originate in dark WhatsApp groups often jump to public platforms. Our algorithm automatically posts counter-narratives and verified fact sheets to X (Twitter) with authoritative regulatory citations.
